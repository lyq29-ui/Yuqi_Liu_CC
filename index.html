A link to a creative code project or artist Behnaz Farahi

This is a website of artist Behnaz Farahi. Many of his works explore the intersection of social issues and the human body.

What I find interesting about this artist work is: The reason I like him the most is that he's very skilled at using installations to express abstract concepts—such as gazes, emotions, and time... Through his installations, he visualizes these elements that don’t exist in physical space, conveying his perception of society to the audience. My favorite piece of his, Caress Of The Gaze, He uses an infrared device to track a person's gaze; when the gaze focuses on a particular spot, the scales on the device also converge there, visualizing the line of sight.It combines human gaze with sensors and coding to express his perspective on social issues between people.
